# 🚐 CyberVan 2035 — Designing the Future Autonomous Family Mobility Experience

> Reimagining autonomous mobility through AI, Human-Centered Design, Industrial Design, Digital Twins, and Future Family Experiences.

![Cover](docs/images/cybervan-hero.jpg)

---

## Overview

**CyberVan 2035** is a speculative design research project exploring how autonomous vehicles will evolve from transportation into intelligent living environments.

Instead of designing another vehicle dashboard, this project investigates how AI, Human-Machine Interaction (HMI), Digital Twins, accessibility, spatial design, and autonomous systems can create a seamless mobility experience for families.

The concept combines:

- Product Design
- UX Design
- Automotive HMI
- Industrial Design
- Interior Experience
- Service Design
- AI Experience Design
- Motion Design
- Accessibility
- Design Systems
- Human-AI Collaboration
